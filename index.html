Om Traders

Om Traders is a trusted name in aluminium and glass fabrication, specializing in premium quality sliding windows, aluminium partitions, toughened glass installations, and modern bathroom doors. With years of expertise in the industry, we deliver custom solutions for both residential and commercial projects in Talegaon Dabhade and surrounding areas.

We combine expert craftsmanship with quality materials to provide durable, aesthetically pleasing solutions that enhance spaces while ensuring functionality and safety. Our focus on precision installation and customer satisfaction has made us the preferred choice for aluminium and glass work in the region.
