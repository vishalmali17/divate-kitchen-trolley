About Divate Kitchen Trolley

Premium Modular Kitchen Solutions Since 1999

Divate Kitchen Trolley is a trusted name since 1999 in modular kitchen fabrication, specializing in premium quality Post Form Shutters, Acrylic Shutters, PVC Shutters, Kitchen Cabinets, and all types of modular furniture. With a commitment to quality materials, precision craftsmanship, and timely completion, Divate Kitchen Trolley transforms ordinary kitchens into statements of beauty, functionality, and performance.
