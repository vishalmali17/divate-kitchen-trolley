Divate Kitchen Trolley specializes in all types of modular kitchen solutions. Our products include Post Form Shutters, Acrylic Shutters, PVC Shutters, Kitchen Cabinets, Kitchen Trolleys, and all types of modular furniture. Post Form Shutters offer several advantages: they are durable, moisture-resistant, easy to clean, and provide a sleek modern appearance. They are ideal for kitchen cabinets as they can withstand heat, humidity, and daily wear and tear. We use high-quality post form materials that are available in various colors and finishes.

Acrylic Shutters offer a high-gloss finish with superior durability and scratch resistance, making them ideal for modern kitchens. PVC Shutters are lightweight, moisture-resistant, and budget-friendly, offering excellent value for money. Both options are available in various colors and finishes to match your kitchen design.
